Privacy Policy for Tidebound

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Tidebound, made by Motionz Studios, is an offline fishing game. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

  • Data on your device: your game progress (coins, fish, settings) is saved locally on your device only. Uninstalling the app deletes it. The optional "Save backup" code is created and kept by you.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Reminders: if you turn reminders on, the app schedules notifications on your device. They are created locally and no data leaves your device.
  • Fonts: on first launch the game may download a font from Google Fonts, which can make Google receive your IP address as for any web request.
  • Children: the game does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
